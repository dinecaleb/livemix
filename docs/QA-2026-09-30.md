## P0 — release blockers (open)

### P0-1 SPEAKING / SINGING on the TUNE page freezes the whole mix, MUTE and DIM included — a regression from today

- **Repro:** TUNE workspace, voices panel, press SPEAKING (or SINGING) on any voice. Then move any fader, press
  a channel mute, or press broadcast MUTE.
- **Impact:** the screen changes, the audio does not. Every later `publish()` returns early until something
  re-prepares the device. An engineer pressing MUTE on a howl hears nothing happen. Autopilot, KEEP, BYPASS: same.
- **Root cause:** `cf9e177` gated `MixController::publish()` on `engineHasThisGraph()`
  (`app/native/MixController.cpp:1170`), which compares `role` too (`:1182`). `setInputRole` rebuilds the
  graph with the new role (`:815`), but `MixPage.cpp:2110` and `:2120` never call `services.reconfigure()`
  (TracksPage `:1093` and MixerPage `:1090` do). Same shape on the Assign page (`SetupPages.cpp:1563`).
- **Fix status:** open. Either reconfigure at those call sites or leave `role` out of the gate (a role change
  does not move a strip index, which is the hazard the gate exists for).
- **Regression test:** none. The gate's own test covers an inserted input only.

### P0-2 Opening another session throws away the current one and deletes its autosave

- **Repro:** mix for an hour without Cmd-S (the foot says "autosaved"), then open any other session from the
  sessions list or sidebar menu.
- **Impact:** the unsaved mix is gone with no question asked, and so is the autosave that would have saved it.
  An Untitled session loses everything.
- **Root cause:** `MainView.cpp:1074` and `:2630` → `loadSession` (`Main.cpp:300`) → `openState`, whose first
  line is `autosave.closeCleanly()` (`Main.cpp:322`, which discards the autosave). Nothing saves or asks.
  `newSession` does save first (`MainView.cpp:2470`); open does not. `SESSION-STATE.md` §7 says the document
  is written "before the document is replaced" — no code does.
- **Fix status:** open. **Regression test:** none.

## P1 — must fix (open, new in this pass)

Marked **V** where I re-read the code path myself, **T** where an auditor traced it end to end.

**Live mix / TUNE**
1. **Re-tune keeps an unapproved proposal (V).** While a proposal is on preview the TUNE button is still
   enabled as "Re-tune" (`MixPage.cpp:2044`); `startListening` runs `keepPlan()` (`MixController.cpp:1253`).
   The group-tile TUNE, `MainView::tuneChannel` and Mix Buddy's "TUNE <name>" chip reach it too. The room jumps
   to AFTER and history records it as kept.
2. **A TUNE preview is on air, and emergency moves during it are lost (T; the on-air part V).** Preview is
   `compose()` of the proposal, i.e. the master and every feed; the toast says "Nothing is committed by
   listening". Mute/fader edits during preview go to `kept` and `proposed`, never `plan->before`
   (`MixController.cpp:2352-2370, 2502, 2621, 2630`): on BEFORE a mute is inaudible; on AFTER, a mute is undone by
   BEFORE / REVERT / TRY ANOTHER MIX (`a12bf78` widened the last). One TUNE may move the master trim ±18 dB
   (`StrategyToolkit.cpp:1139`; +5 to +6 dB on QUEENSVIEW), heard at once.
3. **SOLO IN PLACE is one tap on LIVE, even under LIVE SAFE (V).** `setSoloMode` (`MixController.cpp:2250`) has
   no `liveSafeRefuses`; a solo already held goes to the room and stream. A warning toast is the only guard.
4. **Changing the solo (headphone) device takes the broadcast down (V).** `setSoloOutputDevice` → `openWith`
   (`Main.cpp:445/497/532/622`) stops and reopens the broadcast at `AudioHost::open`'s defaults, 48 kHz / 64
   samples (`AudioHost.h:39`), re-clocking a 44.1/96 k rig, splitting the take, and `routeOutputs` resets feeds
   to 2, deleting any room/loop feed. `LiveAction::DeviceChange` exists in `LiveSafe.h` and is checked nowhere.
5. **Group mute does not mute that group's effect sends (T; prior audit had P2).** A muted howling LEAD group
   still rings through the plate return into master (`MixEngine.cpp:255-260, 376-383`).

**Recording**
6. **A take is dropped or misplaced when the session changes under it (V for the drop).** Open session, New
   session, Save As, reordering tracks or applying an input map mid-take are all allowed with LIVE SAFE off;
   `stopRecording` silently skips a take whose `trackIndex` is out of range (`DawEngine.cpp:228`) and resolves
   relative file names against the new folder. The WAVs survive on disk; the timeline loses them.
7. **Recover / Keep both leaves the crashed take off the timeline (T).** `recoverUnfinishedTakes` runs during the
   launch restore of the *older* document and deletes the sidecars before the sheet is answered
   (`Main.cpp:340`, `Recorder.cpp:327`); the recovered autosave never gets them.
8. **Record with no inputs "records" silence (V).** `toggleRecord` checks only `isAudioRunning()`
   (`TransportBar.cpp:172`), true for an output-only device (microphone refused, "Not now", console absent).

**Persistence**
9. **Quit while the Recover question is unanswered deletes the crash's work (V).** `saveForQuit` writes the
   document, which is then newer than `.recovered.json`; next launch `check()` deletes the held copy
   (`SessionAutosave.cpp:214`). The new test simulates quit with `closeCleanly()` and misses it.
10. **A failed save says "Session saved." and can leave a truncated document (V).** `SessionStore::save` uses
    JUCE `replaceWithText`, which ignores the write result and renames anyway (`juce_File.cpp:801`);
    `MainView::saveNow` toasts success unconditionally (`MainView.cpp:2577`); autosave failures are silent.
    Mitigated for disk-full-by-recording: the recorder stops with ~30 s of audio left.
11. **The session's devices are overwritten by whatever fallback opened (T).** Capture stores the open device,
    not the chosen one (`SessionState.cpp:22`, `Main.cpp:599`); one launch without the console rewrites the
    session to laptop speakers, output-only.
12. **Renaming one channel refuses every scene, favourite and history checkpoint (T)** — identity is the full
    list of input names (`MixController.cpp:503`).
13. **A crash in a never-saved session is never offered back; every new session is "Untitled" and overwrites
    the last one; Save As never checks for a name clash (T)** (`Main.cpp:578, 278`).
14. **Drum sounds 9-24 snap to sound 8, from today's sanitizer (V).** Spec is an 8-entry choice
    (`ParameterSpecs.cpp:98`), the table holds 24 (`SampleBank.h:49`), applied on every edit and on load.
    Also (T): a missing sound's name is overwritten on the next save, its warning is wiped by
    `recoveryNote.clear()` (`Main.cpp:326`), and Save As / Keep both leave the session's `Samples/` behind.

**Still open from `AUDIT-2026-09-30.md` §K (not re-litigated):** device identity by UID, recorder FIFO
alignment, pre-preamp clip metering, speech-priority detector, group balance on active loudness, BYPASS on air
(master limiter bypassed — confirmed again at `MixEngine.cpp:269`), sample timing. The `ThreadedWriter`
notify lock on the audio thread (`Recorder.cpp:404`, suppressed in `rtsan.supp`) is also still there.

## P2 / P3 (concise)

- P2: a device re-prepare mid-listen leaves TUNE stuck in Listening with Autopilot paused and lamp lit.
- P2: every rebuild/device restart leaks ~20 ms of muted channels and of an engaged broadcast MUTE/DIM
  (`MixEngine::prepare` snaps to `startingPoint`); `reconfigure()` prepares twice; each leaves an unreported
  gap in a running take.
- P2: master limiter can be switched off, and `limiterEnabled`/`bypassAll` load unchecked from a file; master
  feed gain up to +12 dB after the limiter; group feeds never limited — the only ceiling is the 0 dBFS hard
  clip, not true peak. Worst-case gain stack input→group feed is >250 dB, bounded only by that clip.
- P2: Autopilot survives opening another session / reassigning inputs with the old target; fights non-fader
  moves (a channel mute, a macro, a gain cut); acts on one reading after a pause.
- P2: DLIVE always opens at 48 kHz / 64 samples; no rate or buffer control exists, yet the recorder's error
  text points at one. Two-device setups resample the console through the aggregate's drift correction.
- P2: a stopped take (disk, rate change) is announced by a passing toast only; one Space/R stops the service
  take with no confirmation, LIVE SAFE included.
- P2: telemetry is on by default with no first-run notice; the Supabase global rate limit lets one key-holder
  lock out all installs and grow storage ~240 MB/h.
- P2: moving the solo feed under LIVE SAFE can unroute the room feed (`onlyMonitorChanged` + collision rule).
- P2: two Autopilot tests cannot fail (3 s / 2 s runs, under the 8 s learn); benchmark gate has no MixEngine
  row and its baseline is not from the CI runner.
- P3: developer source path compiled into the release binary (`DLIVE_SAMPLES_DIR`; runtime falls back to the
  bundle correctly); crash handler uses `backtrace_symbols_fd` (not async-signal-safe); OpenAI key in plaintext
  XML; feed gain/mute step without a ramp; `TimelinePlayer` seek race; at minimum window width the toolbar
  hides transport and DIM/MUTE/BYPASS/AUTOPILOT state (the foot still shows engine/recording state).

## Performance (measured, offline only)

`livemix_benchmark`, one core, 48 kHz: 48 channel strips at 64 samples = 12.0 % of the block budget, at 256 =
13.7 %; 106 sample-replacement instances ≤ 1.5 %; 16 FX chains ≤ 5.6 %. There is no whole-graph (MixEngine +
recorder + meters + UI) measurement, no memory or thread-count figure, no dropout count and no soak.
**Maximum tested channel count: 48 (benchmark), 21 inputs / 15 files (real service, offline).** Latency is
reported, not measured: 72 samples at 48 kHz for the master limiter.

## Audio reliability

No dropout or glitch testing was possible without a device. From code: the process path neither allocates nor
locks apart from the known recorder notify; NaN and full-scale guards are sound (no fast-math on the engine).
Routing faults found are all control-side (P0-1, P1-3/4/5).

## Recording reliability

Format, header flush every 15 s, sidecar every 20 s, crash repair, disk-full stop and the raw tap are sound in
code; JUCE switches to RF64 past 4 GiB on a clean stop, but crash repair refuses RF64 (a >4 h stereo take
crashed mid-way is not auto-restored). No recording was run in this pass. Loss happens above the recorder
(P1-6/7/8).

## Persistence / recovery

Round-trip of every field is tested and passes. The write path is sound for the autosave; the *decisions* about
when to write lose work (P0-2, P1-9..14).

## TUNE MIX — PARTIALLY VERIFIED

Deterministic and idempotent on a real service at four windows; lands -14.9..-15.5 LUFS on the first pass and
converges on a second fresh listen; -1.0 dBTP held. Fails on the approval model: preview on air, Re-tune keeps
without KEEP, edits during preview lost (P1-1/2).

## AUTOPILOT — PARTIALLY VERIFIED

Closed-loop test passes; bounds (±4 dB, 0.5 dB / 400 ms audio), NaN guard, frozen-meter hold, hand-back on fader
touch, one-press off and not persisted all hold in code. Two of its tests are vacuous; it carries stale state
across a session change and reacts to non-fader moves (P2).

## MIX BUDDY — PARTIALLY VERIFIED

A question never changes the mix (tested); answers name real labels. Its "TUNE <name>" chip reaches the
auto-KEEP of P1-1, and "Propose it" is heard on air before KEEP (P1-2).

## Production build

Not tested as shipped: no signed/notarised artefact, no install, no launch, telemetry compiled out.

## Remaining risks

Everything that needs hardware or time: device loss and return on the real rig, two identical interfaces,
aggregate/solo-device behaviour when one piece vanishes, CoreAudio overloads at 64 samples on a laptop with
32+ strips, a 3-hour take, a real disk filling, the live network dropping with telemetry configured.

## Release decision

**NOT READY FOR PRODUCTION.**

Two P0s are open, and one of them (P0-1) was introduced by today's hardening: a normal button on the TUNE page
silently disconnects every control from the audio, MUTE included. The other (P0-2) loses a whole unsaved mix on
an ordinary Open. Behind them sit fourteen new P1s — most in the approval model of TUNE, LIVE SAFE's coverage of
solo and devices, and how takes and sessions survive everyday actions — plus the seven P1s the first audit left
open. None of the hardware-dependent paths (device loss, long recording, disk full, soak) has been exercised on
this build at all. The test suites are green, and that is the false confidence the brief warned about: none of
the P0s, and few of the P1s, is reachable from any existing test.

Before a live service: fix P0-1 and P0-2 with tests; fix P1-1..4, 6, 8, 9, 10, 14; rehearse on the real rig
(unplug/replug, 3-hour take, solo device change) with the signed build; then re-run this pass.
